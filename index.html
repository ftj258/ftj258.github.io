Why digital forensics

I've always loved a mystery

The kind where you follow evidence backward until the full picture makes sense. Crime scene investigation and forensic cases fascinated me, but the physical side never suited me — I'm not someone who can deal with an autopsy or blood. So I found the version of that work I could actually do: digital forensics. The crime scene is a laptop or a phone instead of a physical location, the evidence is login activity and data instead of fingerprints, and what I'm looking for is how an attacker got in rather than how someone got into a room.
